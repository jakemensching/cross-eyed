Hey, I'm Ashtyn

I grew up near Cadillac, Michigan, and these days I'm at Kuyper College playing basketball and working on degrees in business and theology. When I'm not in class or on the court, I'm outside: running, hiking, hanging by a river, and yeah, jumping off the occasional bridge. Into deep water. Usually on purpose.

I love Jesus, I love people, and I love a good thrift store. I believe caring for what God made matters, from the rivers and woods to the stuff sitting in our garages. So I take old rugs and carpets headed for a landfill and turn them into hats. Kind of like what He does with us: takes old things and makes them new.

Every hat is one of one. If you're wearing it, nobody else is. Hit me up, tell me a joke, and let's find yours.
